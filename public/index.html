HOW TO PLAY

AJAN wants you trained and well fed. First, find and finish the 7 training exercises hidden in the Exercise Rooms around the school: Order Assembly, Cash Register, Kitchen Timing, Ingredient Selection, Food Preparation, the Training Quiz and Quality Control.

After all 7, cash ($1-$4 bills) appears on desks, tables and in hallways. Collect it, then walk to the Food Court north of the cafeteria. Buy food at the KFC or Popeyes counter until you reach 7,000 calories.

Then AJAN loses it. Everything turns red. Run to an EXIT in a corner of the school!

Careful: once you finish your first exercise AJAN starts hunting you. Listen for his STOMPS - they get faster as you progress. Opening doors makes noise.

Characters

  • Principal - no running in the halls, no students in the Faculty room. Breaking a rule in front of him = detention.
  • Playtime - makes you jump rope 5 times. Press SPACE when the rope hits the floor. Scissors cut the rope.
  • Bully - blocks a hallway and takes one of your items.
  • Gotta Sweep - sweeps everyone down the halls.
  • 1st Prize - turns slowly, then charges and shoves you. Scissors cut his wires.
  • Arts and Crafters - shy... until you finish all 7 exercises. Don't stare at him.

Controls

WASD move · Mouse look · SHIFT run · LEFT CLICK interact · RIGHT CLICK use item · 1-3 / wheel select item · ESC pause

Items

Safety Scissors, Shiny Quarter (vending machines), AJAN Cola (push characters), Principal's Keys (escape detention), Protein Bar (stamina), Swinging Door Lock, Quiet Sneakers, Alarm Clock (distraction), Energy Drink (unlimited sprint).
